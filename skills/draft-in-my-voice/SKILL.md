# Draft in my voice

Read AGENTS.md and docs/writing.md. Inputs: writer, reader, purpose, channel, intended next action, permitted evidence and an approved private voice guide. No company-specific style or strategy is embedded here.

1. Confirm data permissions and retrieve the relevant voice topic and factual context. If no voice guide exists, use plain writing defaults and propose a guide from permitted samples.
2. Draft to the reader's decision and channel length. Keep claims within the evidence; never invent personal experience, results, endorsements or urgency.
3. Check tone against the approved examples. Replace vague praise and filler with concrete detail. Preserve meaningful uncertainty.
4. Check names, links, dates, factual support and requested next action. Provide the text as a draft for the writer to review and send.

Output: one concise draft, with factual questions separately identified. Do not send, publish, schedule delivery or alter the approved voice guide directly.

Synthetic example: a coach wants a short session recap. Use the agreed next action and date, with client-sensitive details only under the correct processor policy.

Missing-data case: if the next appointment is unconfirmed, leave it unresolved rather than writing a date. If the writer has not approved samples for external processing, do not give them to a cloud model.
