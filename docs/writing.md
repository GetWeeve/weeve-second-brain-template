# Write clearly, sound like yourself

Give the reader a useful point early. Use concrete verbs, short connected paragraphs and the vocabulary they use. Explain a necessary technical term once. Match length to the decision the reader needs to make.

## Editing checks

- Replace unsupported praise with a specific observation and its source/date.
- Remove openings such as “in today's fast-paced world” and endings that repeat the whole piece.
- Replace vague verbs such as “leverage” with the action: read, use, compare, record, decide.
- Avoid invented metrics, testimonials or personal experiences. Mark inference and uncertainty.
- Remove fake contrasts, repetitive three-part slogans, unnecessary headings and decorative emphasis.
- Prefer one concrete example to a string of broad promises. Keep qualifiers that affect accuracy.
- Vary sentence length naturally. Do not add deliberate mistakes to appear human.
- Check names, claims, links, audience and the next action. Keep outbound messages as drafts.

These are editing heuristics, not a reliable test for whether a person or model wrote something.

| Before | Clearer draft |
|---|---|
| “Our revolutionary workflow unlocks seamless productivity.” | “The weekly review puts proposed changes in one decision list.” |
| “Everyone loved the session.” | “Three of five respondents asked for another session. Two did not reply.” |
| “It is important to note that the figures may potentially differ.” | “The export excludes trial accounts, so the totals differ.” |

## Capture a private voice guide

Ask for three permitted writing samples and the intended reader. Store them in the private workspace, under their permitted processor policy. Propose a short wiki guide using this form:

```text
Audience and purpose:
Preferred length and structure:
Words and phrases I use:
Phrases I avoid:
How I express disagreement and uncertainty:
One approved before/after example:
Claims that always need evidence:
Outbound review owner:
Source samples and dates:
```

Have the writer approve the guide. Keep factual company knowledge in its own topics and task steps in a skill. Retrieve only the relevant voice guide and evidence while drafting. See [the generic voice skill](../skills/draft-in-my-voice/SKILL.md).
